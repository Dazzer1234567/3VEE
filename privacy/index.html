Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026 (accounts and sign-in added)

This explains what happens to your details when you sign up for the MASTERDAW beta on this website. The short version: we only use them to send you the download link and to understand how the beta is going. We never sell your details or use them for advertising.

Who we are

MASTERDAW is made by 3vee, based in the Netherlands. For anything about your data, email support@3vee.app.

What we store

When you sign up for the beta, we create an account for you and store:

  • Your email address, to send you the link.
  • Your name, only if you fill it in.
  • Which platform you chose (Windows, Mac or Linux).
  • Your internet (IP) address, and the approximate location it points to (country and city), when you sign up and when you download.
  • When you signed up, whether the email was sent, when you signed in, and when you downloaded.
  • Messages you send us, through your account page or by email to support@3vee.app, including any attachments, and our replies.

When you sign in to your account, we set one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It's needed for the account to work, it isn't used for anything else, and signing out removes it. Browsing the website without signing up or signing in doesn't store anything about you on our server, and we don't use tracking or advertising tools.

Why

  • To send you sign-in links and let you download the beta from your account. We only email you about this, unless you've agreed to more.
  • To answer your support questions.
  • To protect the sign-up form from abuse, for example by limiting how many requests come from one address.
  • To see how many people try the beta, on which platforms and in which countries, so we know where to focus.

The legal basis is your request for the download (for sending the link) and our legitimate interest in running and improving the beta (for the rest).

Who else handles it

A few services help run the website. They only process your details on our behalf:

  • Vultr: the server that stores the sign-ups, in Amsterdam.
  • Cloudflare: the network the website runs through. It works out the approximate location from your IP address, and receives emails sent to support@3vee.app and passes them to our server.
  • Resend: delivers our emails to you (sign-in links and replies).
  • Google Fonts: supplies the fonts this website uses, so your browser contacts Google when you open the page.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where your details leave the EU, this happens under the safeguards EU law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while the beta runs, and delete them no later than 12 months after it ends, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we store about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Just email support@3vee.app. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
